# Con Market photographic assets and provenance

The site uses recovered photographic assets from its own earlier repository history. The November 2026 restoration brought back market imagery after an interim version used conceptual SVG illustrations.

## Current image inventory

| File | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `public/con-market-hero.jpg` | Restored market entrance photograph, also used by the photo gallery. |
| `public/con-market-hero-documentary.webp` | Earlier site documentary market image. |
| `public/con-market-hall.jpg` | Restored photograph of the covered market interior and food-hall aisle. |
| `public/con-market-spices.jpg` | Restored photograph of dried goods and spice stalls. |
| `public/con-market-hero.svg`, `public/con-market-hall.svg`, `public/con-market-spices.svg` | SVG containers embedding historical photographic image data at the existing image URLs. They are **not** conceptual illustrations in the current version. |
| `assets/real-photos/` | Archived source files retained in the repository. |

The restored photos are displayed on the homepage and the four-language photo gallery (`/gallery/` and localized equivalents). Image alt text describes actual subjects; photo placement should never be described as generated artwork.

## Rights and credit

Presence in an older repository version establishes provenance within the project but **does not by itself establish copyright ownership, re-use permission, or photographer attribution**. Retain the original source files and seek documented permissions or license/credit records before further external redistribution. Do not fabricate a photographer credit or assert original on-site photography by the editorial team.
